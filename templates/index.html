This is a small reader circle helping refine the content and reading experience of Morning Mercies. We are asking for honest feedback about the stories, biblical clarity, prayer, page design, reading length, and whether the devotion actually stays with you after you close it.

We are not asking for praise. We are asking for truth offered with grace.
