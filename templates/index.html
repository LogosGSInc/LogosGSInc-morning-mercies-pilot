What we are building

An undated 30-day devotional you can begin when you need it.

Each finished devotion is being tested first as a web reading experience, then refined toward a clean two-page print spread: story, Scripture, blessing, and prayer. The goal is not to produce more content. The goal is to produce something worth returning to.

Founding Design Partner Pilot

Read with us for 14 days. Tell us what strengthens you—and what gets in the way.

This is a small reader circle helping refine Morning Mercies before the first 30-day volume is published. We are asking for honest feedback about the stories, biblical clarity, prayer, page design, reading length, and whether the devotion actually stays with you after you close it.

We are not asking for praise. We are asking for truth offered with grace.
