## Panim: Don't Look Away

### A Morning Mercies story on Psalm 27:9 (KJV)

> "Hide not thy face far from me; put not thy servant away in anger: thou hast been my help; leave me not, neither forsake me, O God of my salvation."
>
> — Psalm 27:9 (KJV)

### Story

Denise sat in her car outside the church basement for twenty minutes.

Fourteen months clean.

Then one bad week.

Now she couldn't make herself open the door.

It wasn't the meeting she dreaded.

It was the faces.

A knock came at the window. Bev, her sponsor, stood outside holding two coffees.

Denise kept her eyes on the steering wheel.

"I can't look at anybody," Denise said.

Bev didn't lecture. She set one coffee on the roof of the car.

"Then start with the One who already knows. He's not looking for a reason to turn away from you."

Denise didn't feel brave when she walked in.

She was shaking.

But she walked in.

### Reflection

David prayed this as a servant who knew God's holiness, remembered God's help, and asked for mercy rather than presuming upon it.

Psalm 27 wasn't written about relapse, but someone returning to God after sin can pray these words honestly.

The verse before it gives us God's invitation:

"Seek ye my face."

And David answers:

"Thy face, LORD, will I seek."
— Psalm 27:8

The plea grows out of the invitation.

In Christ, we see the glory of God clearly. At the cross, Jesus bore our sin and its judgment. Those who turn to God through Christ in repentance and faith do not have to hide from Him.

If you slipped this week, don't hide.

Confess it to God.

Then tell one safe person today—a sponsor, pastor, counselor, trusted friend, or someone in your recovery group.

You may be afraid to walk through the door.

Walk through it anyway.

### Prayer

O God of my salvation, You have been my help before.

Don't let me hide from You, and don't let me believe You've turned Your face away from me.

I confess what I've done.

Turn my face back toward You through Jesus Christ, who bore my sin so that I could be welcomed.

Give me courage to walk through the door today.

Amen.

**Let us today: Pray. Repent. Abide.**
