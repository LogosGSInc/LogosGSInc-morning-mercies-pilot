Monday, October 5, 2026

### Scripture

> "Brethren, if any of you do err from the truth, and one convert him; Let him know, that he which converteth the sinner from the error of his way shall save a soul from death, and shall hide a multitude of sins."
>
> — James 5:19–20 (KJV)

### Story

*(Fictional composite story illustrating the message.)*

Caleb had missed five Thursdays. Walt noticed every one.

Walt had been sober twelve years. He knew what an empty chair could mean.

He told his pastor where he was going. Then he drove out and knocked.

Caleb opened the door a few inches. He didn't look good. He didn't look at Walt.

"I'm not coming in," Walt said. "And I'm not here to fuss. Come get breakfast with me."

"Not today."

Walt nodded. "Then I'll knock again Saturday."

He did. That time, Caleb was sitting on the step with his jacket on.

### Reflection

James ends his letter with a job for the church. When a brother or sister wanders from the truth, don't just notice. Go.

"Convert" here means to turn someone back. You can't save anyone. God saves. But He often sends a person to the door.

James warns that sin leads toward death (James 1:15). So he treats turning a wanderer back as a matter of eternal seriousness. His words most naturally point to the wanderer, though Christians differ on the exact meaning of "death" here and on whose sins are covered. What is clear is this: forgiveness belongs to God through Jesus Christ, received by repentance and faith (Ephesians 1:7). Helping someone else does not pay for your own sins.

Go gently. Paul says restore "in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted" (Galatians 6:1). Don't go alone into a place that puts your own recovery at risk.

You can knock. You can't open the door from the outside. Leave the results with God.

Who is missing from your circle? Reach out today.

### Prayer

Father, You came looking for me before I ever looked for You. Show me who has wandered. Give me courage to reach out and humility when I do. Keep me safe while I go. I can't change anyone's heart, but You can. And where I have wandered, turn me back to You through Jesus Christ. Amen.

**Let us today: Pray, Repent, Abide.**
